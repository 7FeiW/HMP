AI for Science & Health

Machine learning for
molecular discovery.

I develop machine learning methods for molecular discovery and computational metabolomics, with applications in drug discovery and human health.

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computing Science at the University of Alberta, supervised by Prof. Russ Greiner and Prof. David S. Wishart.

My main interest is predicting MS/MS spectra from molecular structures to improve the precision and efficiency of mass spectrometry analysis, with applications in metabolomics, drug discovery, biomarker discovery, and environmental analysis. I also study the reverse problem: predicting molecular structures from mass spectra, alongside molecular property prediction, molecular generation, and molecular optimization.

Previously, I was a software engineer at EA and BioWare, working on games such as FIFA and Dragon Age.
